# Principal Bar Rubric

## Case Study Selection Scoring

Score each candidate project 1-5 on each dimension. Projects scoring 30+ are flagship candidates. Projects scoring 20-29 are supporting capsules. Below 20, exclude unless unique coverage value.

### Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 (Low) | 3 (Medium) | 5 (High) |
|-----------|---------|------------|----------|
| **Complexity** | Single feature, clear requirements | Multi-feature, some ambiguity | Multi-system, high ambiguity, competing constraints |
| **Risk** | Low stakes, reversible | Moderate stakes, some irreversibility | High stakes, significant irreversibility, compliance/safety |
| **Cross-functional influence** | Single team, clear ownership | 2-3 teams, some negotiation | 4+ teams, significant alignment work, exec stakeholders |
| **Measurable outcomes** | No metrics available | Some metrics, weak attribution | Strong metrics with clear attribution |
| **Originality** | Standard solution, well-trodden | Some novel elements | Novel approach, pattern-setting |
| **AI judgment** | No AI involved | AI involved, standard application | AI critical, required novel evaluation/guardrails |
| **Evidence strength** | Recollection only | Some artifacts, restricted | Strong artifacts, shareable |
| **Narrative uniqueness** | Common portfolio story | Some distinctive elements | Clearly differentiated, memorable |
| **Confidentiality safety** | Cannot share meaningfully | Can share with heavy redaction | Can share with light sanitization |

### Differentiation Claim Requirement

For each selected case study, write one sentence stating why this story is non-interchangeable with a typical portfolio case.

**Template**: "Unlike typical [category] case studies, this demonstrates [specific differentiator] because [evidence]."

**Anti-pattern**: "This shows my skills in UX design"
**Good pattern**: "Unlike typical enterprise redesign stories, this demonstrates governance under active regulatory scrutiny, with documented policy negotiations and audit trail requirements"

## Role Coverage Check

### Principal Designer Competencies

Must demonstrate at least 2 of:
- [ ] Systems thinking across complex journeys
- [ ] Multi-market or multi-platform complexity
- [ ] Platform/architecture decisions
- [ ] Quality bar setting and enforcement
- [ ] Mentorship and craft elevation
- [ ] Cross-functional influence at senior level

### Head of Experience Design Competencies

Must demonstrate at least 2 of:
- [ ] Team leadership and development
- [ ] Design operations and governance
- [ ] Stakeholder alignment at exec level
- [ ] Resource and roadmap decisions
- [ ] Org-level quality standards
- [ ] Change management

### Senior AI Consultant Competencies

Must demonstrate at least 2 of:
- [ ] AI/ML evaluation and validation
- [ ] Guardrails and safety design
- [ ] Human-in-the-loop patterns
- [ ] Policy and compliance navigation
- [ ] Cost/latency/accuracy tradeoffs
- [ ] Monitoring and rollback design

## Flagship vs Supporting Capsule

### Flagship Case Study (3-5 total)

Full A-J structure. Deep evidence. Complete executive skim + deep dive. Used for primary impression.

**Criteria**:
- Score 30+ on rubric
- Covers at least 2 competencies for target roles
- Evidence tier High or Medium
- Can support 5+ minutes of interview discussion

### Supporting Capsule

Abbreviated structure. Context + problem + outcome + key decision. Used for breadth demonstration.

**Criteria**:
- Score 20-29 on rubric OR
- Covers unique competency gap OR
- Demonstrates range (different domain, scale, or constraint type)

**Capsule template**:
```markdown
## [Project Name] (Supporting)

**Context**: [2 lines]
**Problem**: [1 line, falsifiable]
**Key decision**: [2 lines, the judgment call]
**Outcome**: [1 line, with metric or directional signal]
**Why included**: [1 line, what gap it fills]
```

## Selection Process

1. List all candidate projects with one-paragraph descriptions
2. Score each on rubric dimensions
3. Map to role competencies
4. Identify gaps
5. Select 3-5 flagship studies with best coverage and differentiation
6. Add supporting capsules only if they fill gaps
7. Document exclusion reasons for audit trail
8. Write Differentiation Claim for each selected study
