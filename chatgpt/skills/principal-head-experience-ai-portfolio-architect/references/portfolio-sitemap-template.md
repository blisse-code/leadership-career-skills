# Portfolio Sitemap Template

## Sitemap Structure

```
Portfolio Root
├── Landing / Executive Snapshot
│   ├── Signal strip (role, scope, outcome types, risk class)
│   ├── Positioning statement (ultra-short)
│   └── Persona door navigation
│
├── [Persona Door: Hire Me]
│   ├── Role fit summary
│   ├── Case study quick-nav (filtered for hiring relevance)
│   └── Interview availability CTA
│
├── [Persona Door: Work With Me]
│   ├── Engagement menu (advisory, fractional, sprint)
│   ├── Working agreements overview
│   └── Contact CTA
│
├── [Persona Door: Partner With Me]
│   ├── Research/teaching agenda
│   ├── Collaboration formats
│   └── Collaboration brief CTA
│
├── Case Studies Index
│   ├── Filterable by: domain, risk, scale, AI involvement, leadership intensity
│   ├── Each entry: title, one-line, outcome type, evidence tier
│   └── 3-5 flagship + supporting capsules
│
├── Case Study Pages (per study)
│   ├── Executive skim block (8-12 lines)
│   ├── Deep dive sections (A-J structure)
│   ├── Evidence table
│   └── Related artifacts link
│
├── AI Integration & Governance Dossier
│   ├── AI philosophy statement
│   ├── Evaluation approach
│   ├── Guardrails and safety patterns
│   └── Case study cross-links
│
├── Leadership Operating System
│   ├── Decision-making frameworks
│   ├── Quality gates and review rituals
│   ├── Design governance patterns
│   └── Team development approach
│
├── Artifacts Gallery (sanitized)
│   ├── Organized by type: flows, research, systems, analytics
│   ├── Each with: context line, what it shows, shareability tier
│   └── Gated detail mechanism for restricted items
│
├── Writing & Talks (optional)
│   ├── Published pieces
│   ├── Conference talks
│   └── Teaching/workshops
│
└── Values & Standards
    ├── Anti-claims (what you refuse to do)
    ├── Quality bar definition
    └── Working principles
```

## Navigation Rationale Template

Document why sections are ordered this way based on reader scanning behavior:

| Reader type | First seek | Second seek | Third seek |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| Hiring manager (first pass) | Role fit signal | 1-2 case study skims | Evidence of judgment |
| Hiring manager (deep) | Full case studies | Leadership evidence | AI governance depth |
| Client prospect | Engagement model | Relevant domain work | Working style |
| Academic partner | Methods rigor | Research agenda | Collaboration format |
| Frontier lab reviewer | AI evaluation depth | Systems thinking | Authenticity signals |

## Proof Map Template

|---------|------------------------|---------------|
| Executive snapshot | Outcome metrics, scope indicators | 3 |

| AI governance dossier | Evaluation plans, guardrail docs | 3 |

## Positioning Statement Template

**Ultra-short (1 sentence, max 20 words)**:
```
[Role type] who [core capability] for [audience/domain], with [differentiator].
```
Example: "Design leader who builds governance systems for AI products, with evaluation-first thinking and operational rigor."

**Short (2-3 sentences, max 50 words)**:
```
[Ultra-short]. [Scope of experience]. [Evidence signal].
```
Example: "Design leader who builds governance systems for AI products, with evaluation-first thinking and operational rigor. 12 years across enterprise, consumer, and platform contexts. Led teams shipping to 50M+ users with measurable retention and cost outcomes."

**Long (1 paragraph, max 100 words)**:
Add: specific domains, notable constraints navigated, methodology strengths, and what you bring that others typically miss.

## Two-Speed Reading Design

**Skim layer** (must work standalone):
- H1: Portfolio name + role positioning
- H2: Section titles that carry meaning
- H3: Case study titles with outcome type
- Bullet highlights with metrics or decision signals
- Evidence tier badges

**Deep layer** (accessed on demand):
- Full case study content
- Evidence tables
- Artifact galleries
- Appendix packs

**Rule**: Reader can complete skim layer without scrolling into deep layer by accident.

## Anchor Syntax Standard

Use consistent slug rules for TOC links:
- Lowercase, hyphen-separated
- Pattern: `#section-name` or `#case-study-slug`
- Example: `#ai-governance-dossier`, `#case-study-enterprise-sso-recovery`

## Growth Principle

Design sitemap to accommodate:
- Adding 1-2 more case studies without restructuring
- Adding new artifact categories
- Adding new sections (e.g., "AI Safety Research" for frontier lab targeting)

**Do not** create deeply nested structures that become unwieldy at scale.
