# Quality Rubric

Score each 1-5. Anything under 3 must be repaired before delivery. Report the scores in one compact table.

| # | Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Answer first | Skim alone conveys level, scope, outcome, hardest call | Outcome appears at the end, if at all |
| 2 | Mandate clarity | Reader knows what was at stake and who asked | Starts with "The client wanted a redesign" |
| 3 | Decisions over deliverables | 3-5 decisions with options, trade-offs, dissent | Process diary |
| 4 | Leadership leverage | Up, across, and down moves visible with artifacts | Only personal craft or only vague "aligned stakeholders" |
| 5 | Attribution | Responsibility map; "I" vs team is exact; team credited | "We" everywhere or "I" for team outcomes |
| 6 | Messy truth | Real conflict, concession, failure, and what changed the user's mind | Frictionless success |
| 7 | Business link | Outcome in business terms with baseline, timeframe, counter-metric | "Users loved it" |
| 8 | Seniority signals (Yang) | Initiative, product thinking, scope, one envelope-pushing move | Isolated feature work |
| 9 | Balance (Seo) | Both sides of the five pairs across the case set | One-sided |
| 10 | Evidence integrity | Every claim in evidence table; no invented numbers | Unsourced numbers |
| 11 | Plain language | Outsider can follow; headings tell the story | Jargon, label headings |
| 12 | Player-coach | 1-2 visible craft moments | Leader has vanished from the work, or never left it |

## Skeptic pass
1. List the 5 claims easiest to shoot down.
2. For each: add evidence, narrow the claim, add attribution limits, or remove it.
3. Log: "Claim, attack, repair".

## Red flags (fix before delivery)
- A leader who never disagreed with anyone.
- Outcomes without baselines.
- Rituals listed without effect.
- AI mentioned without guardrails or evaluation.
- Confidential client or numbers exposed.
- Em dashes or banned words.
