## 1. Core formulas

**XYZ (Laszlo Bock, Google):** Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z].
> Raised mobile checkout completion from 61% to 74% in two quarters by leading a redesign of payment and address flows across 3 squads.

**CAR (Challenge, Action, Result), best when the mandate matters:**
> Inherited 4 inconsistent country apps with no shared components; built a regional design system and governance council; cut new-feature design time by roughly a third and unified UI across 4 markets.

**Scope-first (for org building):**
> Built the experience design practice from 3 to 14 (design, research, content) in 18 months; introduced a career ladder and critique cadence; 4 promotions, 0 regretted attrition.

**NN/g UX variant:** [Verb] [quantitative outcome] [context] using [skill/method].
> Increased new-customer acquisition 20% for a telco e-shop using journey mapping and weekly usability testing.

Pick the formula per bullet; don't force one shape across a whole role (rhythm monotony reads as template).

## 2. Leadership verb bank by dimension

Choose the verb that matches what the user really did. Verb honesty is attribution honesty.

| Dimension | Verbs |
|---|---|
| Business outcome | Grew, raised, cut, reduced, recovered, won, retained, expanded, doubled |
| Team and org building | Built, hired, scaled, restructured, founded, grew, stood up |
| Operating model | Introduced, instituted, redesigned (a process), standardised, governed, streamlined |
| Cross-functional influence | Aligned, secured, negotiated, convinced, partnered with, brokered, reframed |
| Strategy and vision | Set, defined, charted, authored, steered, prioritised |
| Quality bar and craft | Raised, audited, designed (hands-on), prototyped, shipped, enforced |
| Innovation and AI | Launched, piloted, introduced, automated, productised |
| Talent growth | Coached, mentored, promoted, trained, developed |

Avoid "responsible for", "helped", "worked on", "involved in", "participated in": they hide the role.

## 3. When there is no number

Never invent. Use, in order of strength:

1. **A real number the user can find** (ask: dashboards, QBR decks, award entries, performance reviews).
2. **A safe range or direction** labelled as such, if the user confirms: "roughly a third", "from bottom to top quartile [directional]".
3. **Scale markers** (true counts): markets, products, users, team size, stakeholders, releases, clients.
4. **Mechanism + observable change**: "moved sign-off to a weekly design council, ending the 3-round exec rework loop".
5. **Adoption**: "framework adopted by 5 other product teams".
6. **Third-party signal**: award, client renewal, contract extension, analyst mention.

Mark every unverified number `[Need number]` in the evidence log, and keep the placeholder visible in the draft until the user confirms.

## 4. Attribution language

| Situation | Language |
|---|---|
| User did it personally | Designed, wrote, prototyped, ran, facilitated |
| Team did it under user's direction | Led the team that..., directed..., set direction for... |
| Shared with peers | Partnered with product and engineering to..., co-led... |
| User influenced but didn't decide | Made the case for..., proposed and won approval for... |

## 5. Weak to strong rewrites

| Weak | Why weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Responsible for managing the design team | Duty, no scale or result | Led 8 designers across 3 squads; introduced weekly critique and a review gate that cut late-stage rework `[Need number]` |
| Improved user experience significantly | Vague, unfalsifiable | Reduced onboarding drop-off from 42% to 27% by rebuilding identity verification with risk and engineering |
| Passionate about mentoring | Adjective claim | Mentored 5 designers; 3 promoted to senior within 18 months |
| Worked with stakeholders to align on vision | Every leader says this | Replaced 4 competing roadmaps with one experience north-star, adopted by CPO as H2 planning input |
| Led design system initiative | Label only | Built and governed a design system used by 40+ designers and 6 engineering teams; component reuse `[Need number]` |
| Used AI tools to improve efficiency | Hype-adjacent | Introduced AI-assisted prototyping to the team; concept-to-test time down from ~2 weeks to ~3 days `[Need confirmation]` |
| Managed client relationships | Duty | Led UX on a telco account through 2 contract renewals; expanded scope from 1 to 3 product streams |

## 6. Banned and discouraged words

Banned (hollow or AI-marker): results-driven, dynamic, passionate, visionary, synergy, leverage (as a verb for "use"), seamless, delighted users, game changer, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class, spearheaded (overused), orchestrated (overused), holistic, robust, innovative (as self-description), thought leader, rockstar, ninja, guru, go-getter, detail-oriented, team player, significant improvement, delve, tapestry, elevate, unlock, empower.

Discouraged: responsible for, helped, assisted, various, multiple (give the number), successfully (implied), strategic (show it).

## 7. Mechanics

- No em dashes; use commas, colons, or periods.
- No first-person pronouns in bullets.
- Past tense for past roles; present tense for current role.
- Numbers: numerals for everything 10+ and all metrics; consistent % and currency format (state currency).
- Acronyms: spell out once if the recruiter might not know them (DesignOps, NPS is fine).
- 1-2 lines per bullet. If a bullet needs 3 lines it is two bullets or it belongs in a case study.
- Order bullets by relevance to the Target Map, then by impact.
