## Northlight Digital (agency), Singapore — Head of Experience Design, Mar 2022 – present
- Lead the XD practice: 11 people (7 product designers, 2 researchers, 1 content designer, 1 DesignOps). Grew it from 4 when I joined. Report to the Managing Director.
- Accounts: a regional telco (confidential), a Malaysian bank (confidential), an insurance client. Around 3-4 active accounts at a time.
- Telco account: they had 3 separate country apps (SG, MY, ID) built by different vendors, inconsistent. I pushed for a shared design system instead of redesigning each app. Client PO wanted a quick reskin for launch; I negotiated a pilot in MY first. System now used across all 3 apps. Not sure of exact adoption %; the client said design-to-dev handoff got "way faster". Contract was renewed twice (2023, 2024) and expanded from 1 to 3 workstreams.
- Bank account: onboarding app; research showed drop-off at document upload. I recommended fixing upload flow before visual redesign. Completion in pilot went from 58% to 71% over one quarter (client dashboard, I have the screenshot).
- Introduced weekly critique, a design review gate before dev handoff, and a career ladder for the team. 3 people promoted (2 to senior, 1 to lead). One senior designer left in 2023 (burnout on the telco account; I rebalanced workloads afterwards).
- Started an internal "AI in design" session series; team now uses AI for research synthesis first drafts and rapid prototyping. No hard numbers; prototype turnaround feels ~2x faster.
- Still personally design critical flows (did the bank's upload flow myself).
