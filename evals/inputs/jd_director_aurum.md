Director of Product Design — Aurum Pay (fictional fintech), Berlin, Germany

About the role
Aurum Pay serves 4M small businesses across Europe with payments and invoicing. We are looking for a Director of Product Design to lead our 18-person design team (product design, research, content design) across three product lines. You will report to the CPO.

What you'll do
- Lead, grow, and coach a team of designers and design managers; hire to a high bar
- Set the design vision and strategy across payments, invoicing, and onboarding
- Partner with Product and Engineering leadership on roadmap and priorities
- Scale our design system and design operations
- Establish how we measure the impact of design on business outcomes
- Drive AI-assisted product experiences responsibly
- Stay close to the craft; review and raise quality

What you bring
- 10+ years in product design, 5+ in leadership roles
- Experience leading managers or multi-disciplinary teams
- Fintech, banking, or regulated-industry experience
- Track record of design systems at scale
- Strong stakeholder management with C-level
- Data-informed decision making
- Nice to have: marketplace or B2B SaaS experience; German language
