# Leadership Mode Template

## When to Use

Use this template when the user's contribution is leadership rather than hands-on execution:
- Managing teams
- Setting direction
- Establishing quality standards
- Stakeholder alignment
- Governance and decision-making
- Coaching and development

---

## Responsibility Map (Required)

Clearly distinguish your contribution from the team's:

```markdown
## Responsibility Map

### Owned (directly responsible)
- [Deliverable/decision 1]
- [Deliverable/decision 2]

### Led Through Others (set direction, others executed)
- [Area 1]: Team members did [execution], I provided [direction/oversight]
- [Area 2]: Team members did [execution], I provided [direction/oversight]

### Influenced (advocated, did not decide)
- [Area]: Advocated for [position], decision made by [who]

### Consulted (provided input, not decision-maker)
- [Area]: Provided [input type], decision made by [who]

### What I Did NOT Do
- [Activity 1]: Done by [who]
- [Activity 2]: Done by [who]
```

## Role Clarity Enforcement

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Claim type | Problem | Fix |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| "I designed..." | Implies hands-on when you led | "I directed the design of..." or "The team designed..." |
| "We shipped..." | Vague attribution | Specify role: "I led the team that shipped..." |
| "I improved..." | Implies sole credit | "Working with [team], improvements included..." |
| "Responsible for..." | Ambiguous | Use responsibility map: Owned/Led/Influenced/Consulted |

### Good Attribution Examples

**Team execution, your direction**:
"I set the design direction for the checkout redesign. The team executed across 6 sprints, with my involvement focused on quality gates and stakeholder alignment."

**Collaborative decision**:
"The decision to prioritize mobile was made collaboratively with Product and Engineering. I advocated for this based on analytics showing 70% mobile traffic."

**Your execution**:
"I personally conducted the stakeholder interviews and synthesized findings into the prioritization framework."

## Measurable Leadership Outcomes

| Outcome type | Examples | How to measure |
|--------------|----------|----------------|
| Throughput | Velocity, cycle time, shipping cadence | Before/after comparison |
| Quality | Defect rate, redesign rate, stakeholder satisfaction | Before/after comparison |
| Team growth | Promotions, skill development, retention | Named examples (sanitized) |
| Alignment | Decision speed, escalation rate, rework | Before/after comparison |
| Influence | Adoption of frameworks elsewhere, requests for involvement | Named examples |

```markdown

- **Decisions I owned**: [List]
- **Decisions I influenced**: [List]
- **Decisions delegated**: [List]

- [Activity 2]

### RACI Summary
| Activity | R | A | C | I |
|----------|---|---|---|---|
| [Activity 1] | [Role] | Me | [Roles] | [Roles] |
| [Activity 2] | Me | [Role] | [Roles] | [Roles] |
```
