# Persona Doors Navigation

## Intent Router System

The portfolio must support three explicit entry points that route readers to relevant content without forcing them through irrelevant sections.

---

## Door 1: Hire Me (Hiring Manager)

### Landing Section (1 screen)

**Promise**: What value you bring to their team
**Proof**: 2-3 signal chips (outcomes, scale, domain fit)
**Next click**: Direct path to most relevant case study

```markdown
## For Hiring Managers

**I bring**: [Core value proposition in 1 sentence]

**Evidence**:
- [Outcome signal 1]
- [Outcome signal 2]
- [Domain signal]

**Start here**: [Link to strongest case study for hiring context]
```

### Curated Path

| Step | Content | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Case study most relevant to role | Demonstrate core competency |
| 2 | Leadership operating system | Show how you work |
| 3 | Second case study | Breadth demonstration |
| 4 | Values and standards | Culture fit signal |

### What Hiring Managers Care About

| Concern | Evidence to surface |
|---------|---------------------|
| Can they do the job? | Case study outcomes, scope, complexity |
| How do they work? | Operating model, decision-making |
| Will they fit? | Values, working style, collaboration evidence |
| Can they grow here? | Learning, adaptation, self-awareness |
| Are claims real? | Evidence depth, specific metrics, honest reflection |

### CTA

"Available for conversations about [role types]. [Contact method]."

## Navigation Architecture

### Global Index (Filterable)

Case studies accessible from any persona door, filterable by:
- Domain/industry
- Risk level
- Scale (users, markets)
- AI involvement (Yes/No/Core)
- Leadership intensity (Hands-on/Directing/Strategic)
- Evidence strength (High/Medium/Low)

### Cross-Links

Every case study must link to:
- Relevant governance/leadership sections
- Related case studies (if applicable)

Governance/leadership sections must link back to:
- Case studies that demonstrate the pattern

### "Next Best Page" Rules

| Current page | Persona | Next best page |
|--------------|---------|----------------|
| Case study | Hiring | Another case study OR leadership section |
| Case study | Client | Related domain work OR engagement menu |
| Case study | Academic | Methods documentation OR collaboration formats |
| Leadership section | Hiring | Case study demonstrating it |
| Governance dossier | Any | Case study with AI component |

**Rule**: No dead ends. Every page has a clear next action.

---

## Frame-Shift Views

Same facts, different lens. Each flagship case study should be renderable in three views without rewriting the truth:

### Client View

Emphasize:
- Problem framing
- Constraints navigated
- Engagement model implied
- Deliverables
- Risk management
- What they get, how you run the work

### Hiring View

Emphasize:
- Decision quality
- Collaboration
- Execution reality
- Outcome measurement
- Judgment under constraints

### Academic View

Emphasize:
- Methods
- Validity limits
- Theoretical links
- Novel contributions
- What's generalizable

**Rule**: Only the framing changes, not the claims or evidence.

## Gated Detail Mechanism

For NDA-restricted content, implement progressive disclosure:

| Layer | What's visible | Where | Access |
|-------|---------------|-------|--------|
| Public | Redacted narrative, abstracted diagrams | Portfolio | Anyone |
| Recruiter | Sanitized screenshots, ranges, higher specificity | Portfolio (gated) or live screen-share | Recruiter/HM on request |
| Interview | Full artifact walkthrough script | Live interview | Mutual NDA or interview context |

**Gated content rules**:
- Must be optional
- Must not block basic comprehension
- Must have clear access path communicated

## Craft Credibility Layer

Fast-scan proof of taste and precision:

```markdown
## Craft Reel

[6-12 small, high-signal snapshots]

Each snapshot:
- Artifact image (sanitized)
- 1-line "why it exists" caption
- Type tag: [Interaction | Hierarchy | System | Evaluation]
```

**Examples**:
- Microinteraction state sequence
- Information hierarchy example
- System diagram excerpt
- Evaluation artifact sample

**Rule**: NDA-safe by default (restaged or abstracted if needed).

## Depth Router Integration

Every persona door and every case study must support the depth router:

| Time | Content | Works for |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 30 seconds | Signal strip | All personas |
| 3 minutes | Executive skim + 1 proof highlight | All personas |
| 30 minutes | Full case study deep dive | Hiring, Academic |
| 90+ minutes | Appendix pack | Deep diligence |

**Rule**: Reader can complete any tier without accidentally entering the next tier.
