# Interview Story Bank

## Why a bank
Leadership interviews ask 15-30 behavioural questions across rounds. Eight to twelve well-built stories, each tagged to several competencies, cover almost all of them and keep answers consistent across interviewers (panels compare notes).

## Mining prompts (ask in batches, with example answers)
- A time you inherited a mess. What did you fix first and why?
- A time you disagreed with your boss, a PM, or a client and were right. And one where you were wrong.
- A hire you are proud of; a hire or team member who didn't work out.
- A designer you grew. What changed for them?
- A decision you made with incomplete data.
- A project that failed or was cancelled. What did you do next?
- A time you said no to an executive.
- A time you changed how the team works (ritual, process, tool, AI).
- A time you had to deliver bad news up.
- A time you influenced without authority.
- A time you had to balance user needs against business pressure.
- A conflict between two people on your team.
- A moment that shaped how you lead (who-I-am).
- Why design leadership, why now, why this company (why-I'm-here).

## Bank format (one card per story)
```
Title: [memorable handle, e.g. "The 10-week onboarding bet"]
Type: decision / conflict / failure / team build / influence / change / who-I-am
Competencies: judgment, stakeholder mgmt, business acumen, ...
Questions it answers: [list]
Hook line: [the opening moment]
STAR-L bullets: S / T / A (choices + reasons) / R (numbers or observable change) / L
The disagreement: who wanted what
The number: [or mechanism; mark Need number]
Follow-ups to expect: 3 questions + answer bullets
60-second version: [script]
2-minute version: [script]
Evidence: [source; confidence]
```

## Competency matrix
Rows = stories, columns = competencies. Each competency needs 2 stories (interviewers ask "another example?"). Default leadership competency columns:
Vision/strategy · Business acumen · Stakeholder influence (up/across) · Team building/hiring · Coaching/growth · Conflict · Decision under ambiguity · Failure/learning · Quality bar/craft · Change leadership · Cross-functional delivery · Inclusion/culture · AI/innovation judgment

## Common leadership questions to map
- Tell me about yourself. / Walk me through your career.
- Why this role? Why leave your current one?
- What's your leadership style? What would your team say?
- Tell me about a time you had to influence a senior stakeholder who disagreed.
- Describe a time you built or restructured a team.
- Tell me about a low performer you managed.
- Describe your biggest failure as a leader.
- How do you measure design's impact?
- How do you balance craft quality with speed?
- How have you used AI in your team's work, and where did you draw lines?
- What would you do in your first 90 days?

## Answer hygiene
- Answer the question asked in the first sentence, then tell the story.
- Keep 2 minutes as the default; offer depth ("happy to go deeper on how we measured it").
- Never trash former employers or colleagues; describe the situation, not the villain.
- End with the learning or the link to their role.
